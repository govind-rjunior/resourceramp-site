Apply for Cohort 1

For founders.

Twelve months at the Mumbai hub with twelve other startups, a bench of mentors and a group of angel investors who see the company every quarter. Cohort 1 starts in Q1 2027.

Apply for Cohort 1 Fees and equity

What a startup in the programme gets.

Everything an early-stage startup needs and nothing it does not. Founders use what they need and skip the rest.

A dedicated desk at a subsidised rate

A fixed seat at the Mumbai hub with high-speed internet, meeting rooms, printing and the pantry. No upfront programme fee, and no lock-in beyond the programme term.

Three to five mentors for the year

Matched to the stage and the problem, across product, growth, fundraising and operations. A monthly one-on-one with each.

Angel investors attached to the programme

A group of 8 to 12 angel investors who follow the cohort through the year and sit on the demo day panel.

A demo day every quarter

A standard ten-slide template, five minutes on stage, a live investor panel and a room of 30 to 50 people. Four chances a year to be seen.

Vetted partners for the paperwork

Incorporation, compliance, accounting and tax filing, and digital marketing through partners the programme has checked, at preferential rates.

A cohort that talks to each other

Founder Fridays, monthly workshops, skill swaps between teams and a group chat where founders share leads and say what they really think.

A year with a rhythm to it.

The programme is built around an investor engine, not a desk with events attached. Three things happen on a fixed cadence and everything else fits around them.

Every week

Founder Fridays

The whole cohort in one room, talking about what broke that week. A lot of the useful advice in an incubator comes from the founder at the next desk, and this is where it happens.

Every month

Mentor one-on-ones and a workshop

Each team meets its mentors one to one, once a month. One workshop a month covers a subject the cohort needs next: fundraising, product-market fit, legal basics.

Every quarter

Demo day

Five minutes per startup in front of a live investor panel and 30 to 50 founders, operators and investors in the room. Real feedback and real follow-ups, four times in the year.

Who should apply.

Thirteen spots. Resource Ramp is selective because the programme only works if the room is.

Apply for Cohort 1

  • Stage

    Pre-revenue, bootstrapped or seed. An idea with a prototype, or early traction, but not a Series A company looking for an office.

  • Team

    At least two co-founders with complementary skills, usually one who builds and one who sells. Solo founders are considered, with a higher bar.

  • Commitment

    Full-time on the startup, with the team working from the Mumbai hub. The programme is built around people being in the room.

Fees and equity, stated plainly.

An incubator should be clear about how it is paid. This is the deal.

  • What founders pay

    A subsidised rate for the desk. There is no upfront programme fee.

  • What Resource Ramp takes

    A small equity stake in each cohort company, and a success fee on any investment that comes through a Resource Ramp introduction.

  • What stays with the startup

    Intellectual property stays with the startup. A shortlist, a pilot or a demo day does not transfer anything.

  • Where the numbers are

    The exact terms are set out in the term sheet and the written incubation agreement. Every team reads and signs both before it takes its seats.

How to apply.

Five steps from the form to a seat at the hub.

  1. Apply

    Fill in the form or send the same four points by email. It takes about fifteen minutes.

    About 15 minutes

  2. Review

    The programme team reads applications every week and writes back to every founder.

    Reply within 7 working days

  3. Selection

    With the founders' consent, the application goes to programme mentors and the investor panel. Thirteen teams are chosen.

    13 spots a cohort

  4. Onboarding

    Each team reads and signs a written incubation agreement that sets out the terms, then takes its seats.

    Terms in writing first

  5. Incubation

    Twelve months at the Mumbai hub: Founder Fridays, monthly mentor one-on-ones and a demo day every quarter.
