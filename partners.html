Corporates and investors.

Resource Ramp sits between a room of screened early-stage founders and the corporates, lenders and angel investors who want to meet them before everyone else does. The rules of that arrangement are written down here so nobody has to guess.

For corporates

A corporate partner sends written problem statements. Resource Ramp screens the cohort and the wider applicant pool against them, on team, stage and readiness to be onboarded as a vendor, and returns a shortlist of no more than five teams per cycle. Curated, not a dump.

  • Problem-statement screeningUp to five written problem statements per cycle, with a shortlist and a short note on why each team made it.
  • Mentor and judging seatsA seat on the mentor roster, a judging seat at demo days and masterclass slots on what corporates and lenders look for from a young vendor.
  • Pilot preparationDocumentation, data-protection and security answers readied before a two-founder team reaches a vendor-onboarding desk.
  • ReportingA partner report within 21 days of an event, then quarterly through the cohort year, so the relationship can be audited rather than assumed.

For angel investors

The programme carries a group of 8 to 12 angel investors through the year. They see the cohort at four demo days, take mentor seats if they want them, and share deal flow in a private investor group. Resource Ramp is not a fund and does not deploy capital.

  • Four demo days a yearFive minutes per startup, a live panel, and a room of 30 to 50 founders and operators. Follow-ups happen directly between investor and founder.
  • Screened deal flowTeams reach the panel after selection into a cohort of 12 to 13 from a much larger applicant pool, so the room is already filtered.
  • No fees, no carryResource Ramp charges investors nothing to attend, takes no carry on any cheque written and does not sit in the transaction.
  • Mentor seatsInvestors who want to work with a team before deciding can take a mentor seat for the year.

Arm's length, by default.

Four rules apply to every partner, whatever the size of the engagement.

  • Direct contracts

    Every commercial engagement between a partner and a startup is contracted directly, on the partner's paper. Resource Ramp is not a party to it.

  • IP stays put

    Intellectual property stays with the startup. A shortlist, a pilot or a demo day does not transfer anything.

  • No referral fee

    Resource Ramp holds no referral or sourcing fee from any party and stays outside the money flow between partner and startup.

  • Non-exclusive

    No partner pays for anything it has not commissioned, and no startup is tied to a partner by taking part in a screening.
