The Mumbai hub.

Forty seats inside Resource Workspaces, a co-working space that already runs every day. The internet works, the rooms can be booked and the front desk is staffed, so a team can start work on its first morning.

What a founder's desk looks like.

Each team gets fixed seats, not hot desks. The same seat and the same neighbours for the full twelve months.

  • A dedicated seat for every founder on the team
  • High-speed internet and power at every desk
  • Team rooms and open-floor seating
  • The rest of the cohort within earshot

Rooms for the meetings that matter.

Investor calls, customer demos and hard co-founder conversations need a door that closes. The hub has several.

  • A main hall that takes a hundred people for demo days
  • A closed pitch room for twenty, with audio-visual kit
  • Glass meeting rooms that can be booked
  • A boardroom with a screen for investor meetings
