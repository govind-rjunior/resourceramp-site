Apply for Cohort 1.

Thirteen spots, starting Q1 2027. The form takes about fifteen minutes. Applications are reviewed every week and founders hear back within seven working days.

What the form asks for

  1. The companyName, stage, sector and a one-line description of what it does.
  2. The foundersBackgrounds, LinkedIn profiles and why this team is the one to build it.
  3. TractionRevenue, users, letters of intent or a prototype. A link to a one-pager or deck helps.
  4. Why Resource RampWhat the team needs most in the next twelve months and how the programme could get it there.

Founders who would rather write an email can send the same four points to ramp@resource.bz with the subject line "Cohort 1 application". Both routes land on the same desk.

Applications are read by the programme team and, with the founders' consent, shared with mentors and the investor panel during selection. The privacy policy explains how the information is handled.
