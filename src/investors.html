For investors.

Resource Ramp carries a group of 8 to 12 angel investors through each cohort year. They see thirteen screened startups at four demo days, take mentor seats if they want them, and deal with founders directly.

Ask for a seat on the panel See the last investor meet

What an investor gets.

A filtered room, a regular rhythm and time with the teams before a decision has to be made.

  • Four demo days a yearFive minutes per startup, a live panel, and a room of 30 to 50 founders and operators. Decks follow one ten-slide template, with live product and real customer numbers.
  • Screened deal flowTeams reach the panel after selection into a cohort of thirteen from a larger applicant pool, so the room is already filtered for stage, team and commitment.
  • Mentor seatsInvestors who want to work with a team before deciding can take a mentor seat for the year, with a monthly one-on-one.
  • Direct follow-upsAfter a demo day, conversations happen between investor and founder. Any investment is the investor's own decision, on terms agreed with the company.

How Resource Ramp is paid, so nobody has to guess.

Resource Ramp takes a small equity stake in each cohort company and a success fee, paid by the startup, on any investment that comes through a Resource Ramp introduction. Investors on the panel should know that before they hear a pitch.

Resource Ramp is not a fund. It does not manage money for investors, does not pool capital and does not recommend any investment. The disclaimer sets this out in full.
