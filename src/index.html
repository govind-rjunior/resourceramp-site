Operating experience, pointed at early-stage founders.

Resource Ramp is an initiative of Resource Workspaces, the co-working business that already runs the space in Chembur. The front desk, the meeting rooms and the back office exist, so the programme's money goes to founders rather than to rent and reception.

The promoters have spent decades running businesses that have nothing to do with startups, which turns out to be the point. Real estate teaches you about capital that sits still for years. Shipping and logistics teaches you about margins measured in fractions and schedules that cannot slip. Executive search teaches you how to read a person in forty minutes. Chemicals teaches you about regulation, safety and the cost of getting a batch wrong. Founders in the programme get that experience in the room with them, not a slide about it.

The mission is plain enough to fit on one line: help startups that can grow into serious companies, and stay with them through the first hire, the first paying customer and the first institutional cheque. Success is measured by how far the startups go after the programme, not by how many pass through it.

  • Real estateLong-horizon capital, sites, contractors and the patience that comes with them
  • Shipping and logisticsThin margins, tight schedules and working capital that has to turn
  • Executive searchReading people, building teams and knowing when a hire is wrong early
  • ChemicalsRegulated manufacturing, quality control and the discipline of process

A year with a rhythm to it.

The programme is built around an investor engine, not a desk with events attached. Three things happen on a fixed cadence and everything else fits around them.

Every week

Founder Fridays

The whole cohort in one room, talking about what broke that week. A lot of the useful advice in an incubator comes from the founder at the next desk, and this is where it happens.

Every month

Mentor one-on-ones and a workshop

Each team meets its mentors one to one, once a month. One workshop a month covers a subject the cohort needs next: fundraising, product-market fit, legal basics.

Every quarter

Demo day

Five minutes per startup in front of a live investor panel and 30 to 50 founders, operators and investors in the room. Real feedback and real follow-ups, four times in the year.

What a startup in the programme gets.

Everything an early-stage startup needs and nothing it does not. Founders use what they need and skip the rest.

A dedicated desk at a subsidised rate, and no programme fee

A fixed seat in the Chembur hub with high-speed internet, meeting rooms, printing and the pantry. No lock-in beyond the programme term.

Three to five mentors for the year

Matched to the stage and the problem, across product, growth, fundraising and operations. A monthly one-on-one with each.

Angel investors attached to the programme

A group of 8 to 12 angel investors who follow the cohort through the year and sit on the demo day panel.

A demo day every quarter

A standard ten-slide template, five minutes on stage, a live investor panel and a room of 30 to 50 people. Four chances a year to be seen.

Vetted partners for the paperwork

Incorporation, compliance, accounting and tax filing, and digital marketing through partners the programme has checked, at preferential rates.

A cohort that talks to each other

Founder Fridays, monthly workshops, skill swaps between teams and a group chat where founders share leads and say what they really think.

Programme terms, including what each startup and Resource Ramp commit to for the year, are set out in a written incubation agreement signed before a team takes its seats.

  • Stage

    Pre-revenue, bootstrapped or seed. An idea with a prototype, or early traction, but not a Series A company looking for an office.

  • Team

    At least two co-founders with complementary skills, usually one who builds and one who sells. Solo founders are considered, with a higher bar.

  • Commitment

    Full-time on the startup, with the team working from the hub in Chembur. The programme is built around people being in the room.

The hub.

Forty seats inside Resource Workspaces in Chembur, with a main hall for a hundred, a twenty-seat pitch room, glass meeting rooms and the kind of walls people write on.

Rooms that get used

The main hall takes a hundred people for demo days and events. The closed pitch room seats twenty with audio-visual kit for investor meetings.

Everything a desk needs

High-speed internet, printing, a pantry, and meeting rooms that can be booked without a fight. The front desk is staffed.

Chembur, on purpose

On the harbour line and a short drive from the Eastern Express Highway, with a cost base that lets the programme keep the desk subsidy.

For corporates and investors: a screened shortlist, not a cold inbox.

Partners send written problem statements. Resource Ramp screens the cohort and the wider applicant pool against them and returns a shortlist of no more than five teams. Mentor and judging seats, masterclass slots and a standing seat at demo days come with it.

  • Every commercial engagement is contracted directly, on the partner's paper.
  • Intellectual property stays with the startup.
  • Resource Ramp takes no referral fee and stays outside the money flow.
