Who can apply?

Early-stage startups, from an idea with a working prototype through to seed stage. The programme looks for at least two co-founders with complementary skills who are full-time on the company and can work from the Mumbai hub. It is not built for a Series A company looking for an office.

Is there a programme fee?

There is no upfront programme fee. Founders get a desk at the hub at a subsidised rate, three to five mentors, the angel group and four demo days. Resource Ramp's return comes from the equity stake and success fee described in the next answer.

Is equity taken?

Yes. Resource Ramp takes a small equity stake in each cohort company and a success fee on any investment that comes through a Resource Ramp introduction. The exact terms are set out in the term sheet and the incubation agreement, which every team reads and signs before it takes its seats. Intellectual property stays with the startup.

Is funding provided?

Resource Ramp is not a fund and does not write cheques itself. What the programme provides is access: 8 to 12 angel investors attached to the cohort for the full year, and a demo day every quarter in front of a live investor panel. Any investment is a decision between the investor and the founders, and none is guaranteed.

What stage should a startup be at?

Pre-revenue, bootstrapped or seed-funded. An idea with a prototype is enough, and so is early revenue. What matters more than the stage is that the team is working on the company full-time.

Can a solo founder apply?

Yes. Solo founders are considered, with a higher bar. The programme prefers teams of at least two, usually one founder who builds and one who sells, because a year of weekly reviews and investor questions is hard to carry alone.

Is the programme limited to Mumbai-based startups?

The startup can be from anywhere. The team has to be in Mumbai. The programme is built around people being in the room, so founders work from the hub for the twelve months. A team that is not in Mumbai yet can apply if it can be at the hub by the time the cohort starts.

What happens after the programme?

The programme runs for twelve months and the desk carries no lock-in beyond that term. Resource Ramp remains a shareholder in each cohort company, so its interest in how the business does continues after the year ends. Teams that want to keep working from the hub can speak to Resource Workspaces about a desk.
