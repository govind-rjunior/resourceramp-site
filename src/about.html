Operating experience, pointed at early-stage founders.

Resource Ramp is a startup incubation centre in Mumbai and an initiative of Resource Workspaces. Resource Ramp Private Limited was incorporated in July 2026. Cohort 1 starts in Q1 2027.

Our purpose.

Resource Ramp is an initiative of Resource Workspaces, which operates the co-working space where the hub sits. With the workspace, meeting rooms, front desk and back-office support already in place, more of the programme's resources can go towards helping founders build their businesses.

The promoters bring decades of experience running businesses across real estate, shipping and logistics, executive search and chemicals. That experience offers practical lessons for early-stage founders: managing capital through long business cycles, working with tight margins, meeting delivery commitments, hiring well and maintaining quality in regulated industries. Founders will have access to people who have handled these challenges themselves and can help them think through decisions as their businesses grow.

Our purpose is to help promising startups grow into strong, sustainable companies. We aim to work alongside founders through their early milestones, from hiring their first team member and winning their first paying customer to preparing for institutional investment. For us, success is measured by how well these businesses grow and sustain themselves beyond the programme.

What four very different businesses teach.

The promoters' businesses have little in common except that each one punishes a different mistake.

  • Real estateLong-horizon capital, sites, contractors and the patience that comes with them.
  • Shipping and logisticsThin margins, tight schedules and working capital that has to turn.
  • Executive searchReading people, building teams and knowing when a hire is wrong early.
  • ChemicalsRegulated manufacturing, quality control and the discipline of process.
