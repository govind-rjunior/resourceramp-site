For corporates.

A screened shortlist, not a cold inbox. A corporate partner sends written problem statements, and Resource Ramp returns no more than five early-stage teams that can work on them.

Start a conversation The rules of engagement

What a corporate partner gets.

Resource Ramp screens the cohort and the wider applicant pool on team, stage and readiness to be onboarded as a vendor.

  • Problem-statement screeningUp to five written problem statements per cycle, with a shortlist of no more than five teams and a short note on why each one made it.
  • Mentor and judging seatsA seat on the mentor roster, a judging seat at demo days and masterclass slots on what corporates and lenders look for from a young vendor.
  • Pilot preparationDocumentation, data-protection and security answers readied before a two-founder team reaches a vendor-onboarding desk. Pilots run for 30 to 60 days and are paid.
  • ReportingA partner report within 21 days of an event, then quarterly through the cohort year, so the relationship can be audited and not assumed.

Arm's length, by default.

Three rules apply to every partner, whatever the size of the engagement.

  • Direct contracts

    Every commercial engagement between a partner and a startup is contracted directly, on the partner's paper. Resource Ramp is not a party to it.

  • IP stays put

    Intellectual property stays with the startup. A shortlist, a pilot or a demo day does not transfer anything.

  • Non-exclusive

    No partner pays for anything it has not commissioned, and no startup is tied to a partner by taking part in a screening.

How a cycle runs.

  1. Brief

    The partner writes up to five problem statements it wants startups to work on.

  2. Screen

    Resource Ramp checks the cohort and the applicant pool against each statement.

  3. Shortlist

    No more than five teams come back, each with a note on why it fits.

  4. Pilot

    The partner contracts directly with the team it chooses. A report follows within 21 days.

How Resource Ramp is paid.

Resource Ramp takes a small equity stake in each cohort company and a success fee on investments that come through its introductions. Partnership tiers for events and for the cohort year are available on request.
