Saturday, 24 October 2026 · 2 to 6 pm

Local Startups' Meet Mumbai 2026

Eight early-stage founders pitched to six investors at the Bombay Presidency Golf Club, Chembur. The meet brought investors to the founders, in their own part of town, instead of sending founders across the city to find capital.

Resource Ramp partnered with Entrepreneurship Cell, IIT Kharagpur under the aegis of Rajendra Mishra School of Engineering Entrepreneurship for LSM Mumbai 2026. StartupNews.fyi was the Local Media Partner.
