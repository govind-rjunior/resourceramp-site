1. What Resource Ramp is

Resource Ramp Private Limited is a private limited company incorporated in Maharashtra in July 2026. It runs a startup incubation programme from a hub inside Resource Workspaces in Chembur, Mumbai, and provides workspace, mentorship, training, events and introductions to early-stage startups. That is the whole of its business.

2. What it is not

Resource Ramp is not a fund, a bank, a non-banking financial company, an alternative investment fund, a collective investment scheme, a venture capital fund, a stock broker, a portfolio manager, an investment adviser or a research analyst. It is not registered with the Reserve Bank of India or the Securities and Exchange Board of India in any of those capacities and does not carry on any activity that requires such registration. It does not accept deposits, does not pool money from investors, does not deploy capital into startups on anyone's behalf, and does not offer or sell securities to the public.

3. No guarantee of selection, funding or results

Applying to the Programme does not guarantee a place. A place in the Programme does not guarantee a mentor match that works, an investor's interest, an investment, a customer, a pilot, a grant, a recognition, or any commercial result. Investors and partners attached to the Programme make their own decisions in their own time. The Company's role ends at the introduction. Any figures on the Site about seats, cohort sizes, mentors, investors, demo days and attendance describe how the Programme is designed to run and may change.

4. Introductions are not recommendations

When the Company introduces a startup to an investor, a corporate, a mentor or a service partner, it is not recommending that any of them do business with the other, and it is not vouching for either side. Each party is responsible for its own due diligence. The Company does not verify the claims that startups make in their applications or pitches beyond what is needed for selection, and it does not verify the financial standing or intentions of investors or partners.

5. Nothing on the Site is advice

Content on the Site, in workshops, in mentor sessions and at demo days is general information and personal opinion. It is not legal, tax, accounting, regulatory, financial or investment advice, and it does not take account of any particular person's situation. Anyone considering a decision that has legal, tax or financial consequences should take advice from a qualified professional. Service partners introduced through the Programme are independent firms responsible for their own work.

6. Resource Workspaces, and the promoters' other businesses

Resource Ramp is an initiative of Resource Workspaces, the co-working business that owns and runs the Chembur premises, and the two are under common promoters. They are separate businesses. Desk and facility arrangements for cohort startups are provided through the Programme; ordinary co-working memberships at Resource Workspaces are a separate service on separate terms. The promoters' other businesses in real estate, shipping and logistics, executive search and chemicals are described on the Site as background to the operating experience behind the Programme. They are not parties to the Programme, do not sponsor it, and nothing on the Site should be read as an offer of business from them.

7. Events and third parties

Resource Ramp Private Limited is the Venue Partner for Local Startup Meet, Mumbai 2026. The event is organised by another party under a separate arrangement; the Company's role is to host it and to provide the inventory it has agreed to provide. Details about the event, its speakers, partners and audience come from the organiser and may change. Registrations for the event are governed by the organiser's terms. The Company does not control, and is not responsible for, other organisations' websites, content or conduct, including those linked from the Site.

8. Photographs and descriptions

The photographs on the Site are of the Resource Workspaces premises in Chembur where the hub sits. Room layouts, furniture and availability change. Descriptions of the hub are a fair picture of the space, not a specification, and the seat, room and equipment actually made available to a startup are as set out in its incubation agreement.

9. Forward-looking statements

Statements on the Site about cohort dates, the number of places, planned events, future mentors, investors, partners, recognitions or grants are statements of intention at the date they were written. They depend on selection, on third parties and on approvals the Company does not control, and they may not happen as described.

10. Liability

To the extent the law allows, the Company accepts no liability for any loss arising from reliance on the Site or on anything said in connection with the Programme. The limitation and exclusion of liability in the terms of use apply.
